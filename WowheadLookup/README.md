# Wowhead Lookup

Look anything up on Wowhead without leaving **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Alt-click an item, quest or NPC to get its Wowhead link, search the whole game from one window, and see where items come from right in their tooltips.

## Features

**Quick lookups**
- **Alt-click** an item (bags, loot, chat, auction house, professions) or a quest to get its Wowhead link, ready to copy.
- **Right-click an NPC's portrait** and choose **Wowhead**.
- **Share in chat**: send the link to your group with one click.
- **Keybinds** for looking up whatever's under your mouse, your target, or opening search (Esc → Key Bindings → AddOns).

**Search**
- Type **`/wh`** or click the **minimap button** to search every item, quest, NPC, spell, zone and more.
- Filter by type, quality, or what you can use at your level.
- Your recent lookups are always one click away.

**Smarter tooltips**
- Where an item drops, which quests reward it, and what it's used to craft
- The lowest auction house price you've seen
- Quests in your log that need it, with your progress
- Vendors that sell it, and which of your characters can make it
- A heads-up when you get an item for the first time

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/wh` | Open the search window |
| `/wh <name>` | Search for something |
| `/wh target` | Look up your current target |
| `/wh quest` | Look up the quest selected in your quest log |
| `/wh share` | Put your last link in the chat box |
| `/wh minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
| `/wh help` | List every command |

## Install

1. Download the latest release.
2. Unzip it into `\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns`. You should end up with both `WowheadLookup` and `WowheadLookup_Data` in there.
3. Restart the game.
